---
title: Softly, Then All at Once
excerpt: A short poem about the slow arithmetic of missing someone.
category: poems
author: Ifenkili
date: 2026-08-20
mood: Aching
---

It did not arrive the way they warned me,
no flood, no siren, no sky giving way —

it came in the ordinary hours:
the second cup I poured from habit,
the joke I turned to tell the empty chair,
the way I still walk the long side of the bed.

Grief is a landlord.
It lets you keep the rooms
and charges you for every light left on.

So I pay.
I pay in mornings.
I pay in the small coins of your name
dropped, one by one, into the quiet —

softly, softly,
then all at once.
