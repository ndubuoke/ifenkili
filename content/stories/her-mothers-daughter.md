The last time I saw mummy cry,
the tears burrowed a home in her heart long before their descent.
Darkness weighed heavy in the air, her tongue stained with the taste of war and Lot's beloved.
The sofa provided temporary solace for her broken heart, challenging gravity with glassy eyes, to ignore the droplets of her anguish—
of surrender, of relief.
The battle brought forth no victor.
The faint smell of dry catfish still clung to my nostrils,
but tears threatened to be the only ingredient in our evening meal.
How do you instruct the wind to borrow pain on its journey?
or the night to cradle a broken soul?
How much blood would satisfy the thirst of the Earth?
My arms stayed defeated by grief in their quest to comfort this broken child.
For one without a mother is lost.
Let nostalgia soften the ache of death.
