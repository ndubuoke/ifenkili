---
title: What the Kettle Knows
excerpt: A short poem about making tea for two out of habit, months after it became tea for one.
category: poems
author: Ifenkili
date: 2026-09-07
mood: Quiet
---

The kettle still remembers two mugs —
I fill it to the old line from muscle memory,
boil more water than one person needs,
and stand there letting the excess steam
apologise to no one.

I used to time it to your footsteps on the stairs,
mug ready by the second creak.
Now I time it to nothing.
The steam still rises like it's owed to somebody.

There is a specific kind of loneliness
in a kettle too full,
in a fridge with the wrong oat milk,
in a playlist that still skips the song
you didn't like.

I am learning the new line, slowly.
Half the water. Half the noise.
Half the reason to boil it at all.

Some nights I fill it to the old mark anyway.
Not because I've forgotten.
Because forgetting on purpose
felt like a second goodbye
I wasn't ready to make you.
